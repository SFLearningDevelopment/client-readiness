Standards that apply on every assignment

Act honestly, declare conflicts, commit nothing you are not authorised to commit.

Gifts and hospitality. Follow SF standards on what may be accepted or offered. Anything of more than token value, and anything that could look like it is intended to influence a decision, should be declined or disclosed to your Account Manager.

Conflicts of interest. Declare any situation where a personal interest could affect, or appear to affect, your work. This includes outside work related to the client or its competitors, a personal relationship with someone who influences decisions about your assignment, and any financial interest in a party involved in the work.

Timesheets. Record your time accurately, against the correct assignment, and submit on time. Timesheets are the basis of client billing, so an inaccurate entry is not a small administrative error.

Expenses. Claim only what you actually incurred, in line with SF standards, with proper supporting records.

Client relationships. Keep working relationships professional and within SF standards.

Honesty about who you are

Do not misrepresent SF, the client or your role. Describe your role, experience, qualifications and status accurately. Do not present yourself as a client employee, and do not claim authority or seniority you do not hold.

No unauthorised commitments

Do not make commitments on behalf of SF that you are not authorised to make. This connects directly to Module 4: staffing, compensation, contracts, employment terms and commercial matters are for SF management to decide.

Do
  • Follow SF standards on gifts and hospitality.
  • Declare conflicts of interest as soon as they arise.
  • Record time and expenses accurately and on schedule.
  • Describe your role and status honestly.
Don't
  • Accept anything that could look like it is meant to influence you.
  • Leave a conflict of interest undeclared because nothing has gone wrong yet.
  • Enter time or expenses that you cannot support.
  • Commit SF to anything you are not authorised to commit to.

Module recap. Act honestly, declare conflicts, record time and expenses accurately, and make no commitments you are not authorised to make.
