The one idea to carry into every situation

Client manages the work. SF manages the employee.

The client directs your day-to-day work and delivery. SourceFuse owns your employment: pay, leave, appraisal, grievances and escalation. Nearly every question that comes up on a client assignment answers itself once you know which of those two you are dealing with.
